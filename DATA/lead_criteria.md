# Lead Criteria

Criteria are per campaign. See `scoring_notes` in `config/prospecting.toml`.

## Contracting and tree removal
- Has land, buildings, or grounds in Brampton or Prince Edward County
- Buyers: property managers, condo boards, landlords, cottage resorts, farms, orchards, golf courses, and similar
- Not another tree service, landscaper, or contractor
- Not a chain, government office, school, place of worship, hospital, chamber, or board of trade

## Website design
- Independent local business
- No website in OpenStreetMap, or only a weak one
- Phone and address are enough when email is missing
- Not a chain, a web or marketing agency, or the same off-target places as above

## Contact data
- A placeholder, tracking, or role address is cleared. The lead stays.
- The same email or phone on two different names is one lead.
